Looking at your report against the Weber et al. (2019) base paper, here are the key novelties:

**Architecture & Models**
- Base paper uses GCN/Skip-GCN; your report introduces GAT with multi-head attention, which captures weighted neighbourhood relationships rather than uniform aggregation
- You propose SGAT-BC — a novel Subtree Graph Attention Network with two sequential GATConv passes per layer, encoding two-hop context at one-hop computational cost, which has no equivalent in the base paper
- The sigmoid gating mechanism in SGATLayer to combat over-smoothing is entirely new

**Ensemble & Hybrid Stacking**
- Base paper only briefly suggests augmenting RF with GCN embeddings as future work; your report fully implements and evaluates a 273-dimensional hybrid stacking pipeline combining SGAT embeddings, soft probabilities, graph topology features, and baseline predictions fed into CatBoost + XGBoost soft voting
- Bootstrapped bagging of 5 independent SGAT instances for variance reduction is a new contribution

**Class Imbalance Handling**
- Base paper uses simple weighted cross-entropy; your report introduces label smoothing (ε = 0.05) combined with class weighting, plus a dedicated F2-optimised threshold tuning procedure over [0.20, 0.75]

**Graph Feature Engineering**
- Your report introduces 7 leakage-safe topology features (fan-in/out ratio, 2-hop illicit ratio, clustering coefficient etc.) computed strictly on the training subgraph — the base paper has no equivalent

**Explainability**
- Base paper offers only a visualisation prototype (Chronograph); your report applies GNNExplainer with a formal AML typology mapping framework (Fan-out, Fan-in, Layering, Pass-through, Structuring) across 30+ nodes with quantitative feature attribution comparison between GAT and RF

**Training Improvements**
- Cosine Annealing Warm Restarts scheduler, AdamW with weight decay, and gradient clipping are all absent from the base paper
- CatBoost as a baseline (with log-loss + F1 eval metric) is new; base paper only uses LR, RF, MLP, GCN

**Evaluation**
- Base paper reports aggregate metrics only; your report adds per-timestep F1 breakdown across all 15 test steps for every model, with explicit analysis of the dark market shutdown at t=43 rather than just noting it as a challenge
